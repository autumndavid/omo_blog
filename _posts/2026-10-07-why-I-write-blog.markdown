## Practicing my English writing skill
English is the world language. Although I wasn't a native speaker, I can still feel its clarity, logical, and beauty. I had learned English in my primer school, middle school, high school, campus. But that is not the way to learn the real English, even after so many years learning, I still can't talk with foreign. So I decided to learn English as a hobby in a different way -- writing English blogs and getting feedback.
As for writing, I thought English writing could help me a lot in English learning, because the process of writing would force me to think as a native speaker and to figure out the unclear part. It would help a lot.
